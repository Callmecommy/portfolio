02 / Behind the lens

A creative eye.
A purposeful mind.

I’m Comfort, a creative professional who believes every brand has a story worth telling well.

I work across video editing, content creation, mobile videography, social media management, and digital marketing. I transform raw footage into compelling stories and develop ideas that help brands connect with their audiences.

My work goes beyond the final edit. From content planning and creative direction to copywriting and social media strategy, I bring the pieces together with a brand’s identity and objectives in mind.

Working with brands across different industries has shaped how I create: with attention to detail, an understanding of digital audiences, and a clear purpose behind every frame.

I’m always learning, growing, and looking for creative collaborations that bring meaningful ideas to life.
